      South-pole map

      LOLA shaded relief, 80–90°S. Amber = ground NASA Goddard's 18.6-year illumination map (Mazarico et al.) shows lit 15–55 %+ of the time; blue = near-permanent shadow, where water ice can survive. Click anywhere to put Site A or B there — its skyline is computed live from LOLA terrain.

      Rings: 85°S and 88°S. Top of the map faces 0°E (the near side).

      Custom point South of 80°S the skyline is computed live from NASA LOLA terrain (400 m); elsewhere it is labelled geometric-only.

      Real 3D terrain (optional)

      The orbit view can stream Cesium Moon 3D tiles with a free Cesium ion token. Without one it uses NASA Moon Trek LRO imagery. The walkable surface and all science use NASA LOLA data either way.
